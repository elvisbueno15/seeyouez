SeeyouEz Uploader

Developed with YouTube · uses YouTube API Services

Privacy Policy · Terms of Service

SeeyouEz Uploader is a private, internal desktop tool used only by its owner, Elvis Bueno, to upload and schedule his own original videos on his own two YouTube channels:

  • AnimeEz — anime news, hidden details and debates in Spanish.
  • TyroneEz — video game news and hidden details in Spanish.

It is not offered to the public and it does not show YouTube content to anyone. It uses the YouTube Data API only to upload videos (videos.insert), set their custom thumbnail (thumbnails.set) and confirm which of the owner's channels is connected (channels.list, mine=true).

By using this tool, the user agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.
